🗺️ The Grand Adventure Itinerary

Nine days, two countries, three trains, two flights, endless memories. Buckle up!

Day 1 · Sun 11 Oct

🚆 Overnight to Zurich

Pajamas on, adventure begins!

  • 21:02 EC 458 leaves Dresden Hbf
  • Seats: Wg. 257, Pl. 32–36 (family compartment booked 🎟️)
  • Pack: snacks, water, wet wipes, blankets, headphones, tablets, favourite cuddly toy 🧸

Day 2 · Mon 12 Oct

🇨🇭 Hello Zurich → Rhine Falls (Rheinfall)

Europe's largest waterfall on arrival day 🌊

  • 09:05 Arrive Zürich HB — store luggage in station lockers 🧳
  • Train to Schloss Laufen am Rheinfall / Neuhausen (~50 min via Winterthur)
  • View the falls from the Schloss Laufen platforms; boat trip to the rock if running ⛵
  • Return to Zürich — check in from 15:00 at VISIONAPARTMENTS Albertstrasse (self check-in)
  • Upload ID using booking B891286 + last name Chowdhury
  • Quiet hours 22:00–06:00 😴 — light dinner & early night after the overnight train
Flexibility tip: Big first day after a night on the train. If the children are exhausted, do the falls and skip the rest — the waterfall is the highlight.
Day 3 · Tue 13 Oct

🏔️ Lauterbrunnen day trip — Swiss Alps

Valley of 72 waterfalls · SBB day ticket in use 🎫

  • Early start: Zürich → Bern → Interlaken Ost → Lauterbrunnen (~2h 15m each way)
  • Staubbach Falls & the village walk through the Lauterbrunnen valley
  • Trümmelbach Falls: bus 141 from Lauterbrunnen station (~10 min) — glacier falls inside the mountain (bring a light rain jacket!)
  • Optional upgrade: cable car Grütschalp → train to Mürren for Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau views
  • Return to Zürich in the evening — this is a full day out
  • 7-year-old travels free with a parent thanks to the SBB Junior-Karte 👧
Day ticket: Use your SBB day ticket / Saver Day Pass for the whole journey. Must be valid on the travel date and carried with a photo ID.
Day 4 · Wed 14 Oct

✈️ More Zurich → fly to Barcelona

Last Swiss morning, then off to Spain

  • Check out by 10:00 — store luggage at Zürich HB lockers if exploring
  • Zurich highlights close to the station: Old Town (Altstadt), Grossmünster, Lindenhof viewpoint, Lake Zürich promenade 🌅
  • Head to ZRH about 3 hrs before departure (Zürich HB → ZRH ~10–12 min by train)
  • Fly ZRH → BCN (Vueling) 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
  • Transfer BCN → Castelldefels: train, bus, or taxi 🚕
  • Address: Carrer del Doctor Ferran, 5, 08860 Castelldefels
  • Airbnb check-in from 16:00 — keypad self check-in, ask host for code
Days 5–8 · 15–18 Oct

🏖️ Barcelona & Castelldefels Bliss

Sun, sand, tapas and Gaudí magic

  • Castelldefels beach = perfect for little ones 🌊
  • Barcelona by R2 train (~20–25 min):
  • ✨ Sagrada Família · Park Güell · Barcelona Aquarium 🐠 · Ciutadella Park · Gothic Quarter · Montjuïc cable car · Tibidabo
  • Book timed tickets online in advance 📱
  • Stroller for the 4-year-old + carrier for the baby 👶
  • Easy trips: Sitges, Montserrat, or just a lazy beach day ☀️
  • ⚠️ Airbnb shows "4 guests maximum" but you have 5 — confirm with host that the infant is allowed!
Day 9 · Mon 19 Oct

🏁 The Big Journey Home

One last croissant, then homeward bound

  • Check out by 11:00
  • Fly BCN → BER (Vueling)
  • 16:32 IC 2571 departs Berlin Ostkreuz
  • 18:21 Arrive Dresden-Neustadt — home sweet home 🏡
⏰ Critical timing: BER → Ostkreuz takes ~30 min by S-Bahn + walking. The DB ticket is non-refundable and has Zugbindung (train-bound). Give yourself a HUGE buffer — if the flight is late, this train is gone.

📝 Pro Family-Traveller Notes

  • 🔑
    Zurich self check-in: No reception. Upload ID/passport before arrival using booking B891286 and last name Chowdhury. Access code arrives on arrival day.
  • 🌊
    Rhine Falls (12 Oct): Schloss Laufen viewing platforms are the best family viewpoint. Boat trips to the rock only run in season and depend on water levels — check on the day.
  • 🏔️
    Lauterbrunnen (13 Oct): Long day from Zurich (~2h 15m each way). Bring snacks, water, and warm layers — the valley is cooler than Zurich. Trümmelbach Falls is inside the mountain — bring a light rain jacket.
  • 🎫
    SBB day ticket: Used for the Lauterbrunnen trip. Confirm whether it also covers the Rhine Falls route, or buy a separate ticket for 12 Oct. Valid only with a photo ID.
  • 🏠
    Possible apartment swap: Messages mention an alternative at Cramerstrasse 8, 2nd floor. Your reply says no change needed — double-check the final address with VISIONAPARTMENTS.
  • 🚭
    Zurich rules: No smoking, no parties, quiet hours 22:00–06:00, no daily cleaning, toiletries are arrival-only.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦
    Airbnb: Non-refundable. House rules show "4 guests maximum" — confirm the infant is allowed with your host.
  • 🧳
    Flights: Vueling invoices include baggage, but verify the allowance for all 5 passengers. Arrive 3 hrs early.
  • 🎫
    Trains: DB tickets need a photo ID. The Berlin → Dresden ticket is non-refundable with Zugbindung (train-bound).
  • 👧
    Junior-Karte: 7-year-old travels free with a parent holding a valid ticket. Verify SBB rules for the 0 & 4-year-olds.
  • 💱
    Money: CHF in Switzerland, EUR in Spain & Germany. Tell your bank you're travelling and carry a little cash.
  • 📱
    Offline backup: Print all bookings + passports + insurance. Save everything on at least two phones.
